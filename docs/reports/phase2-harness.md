## Finding: the round 1 energy bonus over-corrects in breach mode

The first player wins only 35% in `territorial_breach`. A follow-up run (same 400 seeds, breach mode, measured) isolates the cause:

- `secondPlayerRound1Bonus: 1` (current D-005): first player wins 35%, draws 15%, median 10 rounds.
- `secondPlayerRound1Bonus: 0`: first player wins 53%, draws 16%, median 10 rounds.

So with these placeholder cards the +1 energy for the round 1 second player is worth about 18 points of win rate in breach mode. Unblocked Lane is already at 50% with the bonus. D-005 is unchanged; whether to drop or change the bonus is Morgan's call, best rechecked with the real phase 3 cards.

Draws are 14–16% in every configuration. Both Commanders falling in the same combat is common, because damage is simultaneous and the AI races. This is worth watching in phase 3; it is not a D-008 target.
