## Tuning done in phase 3

Starting stats gave a median of 11 rounds, and Ironbound won 41% in breach mode (200 games). Raising attack from 2 to 3 on Iron Footman, Thornback and Barkhide brought the median to 10 and Ironbound to 48%. The other variants tried, at 200 games each in breach mode:

- **Two attackers, Iron Footman 3/4 and Crossbowman cost 2:** Ironbound 74%.
- **Iron Footman attack 3 only:** Ironbound 67%.
- **Shield Recruit attack 2 only:** Ironbound 58%.
- **Cheap units +1 attack, −1 health:** median 10, but the first player won 59%.

The card list is in `docs/reports/phase3-cards.md`.

## Recheck: the round 1 energy bonus with real cards

Same 400 seeds, both presets, measured (`npm run sim -- 400 1 0` for the no-bonus run):

- **With the +1 bonus (D-005, kept):**
  - breach: first player 166 of 369 decisive games (44.99%, which shows as 45.0%), Ironbound 48%, median 10
  - unblocked lane: first player 42.7%, Ironbound 64%
- **Without the bonus:**
  - breach: first player 50%, Ironbound 55%, median 9
  - unblocked lane: first player 53%, Ironbound 62%

With real cards the bonus is still slightly too strong, but much less than with the placeholder cards: it shifts first-player wins by about 5 points (it was 18). The target range is 45–55%, so breach mode sits right at its lower edge. Keeping or dropping the bonus is Morgan's call.

## Unblocked Lane favours Ironbound

In `unblocked_lane`, Ironbound wins 62–64% with or without the bonus. In that preset any unit with an open lane hits the Commander, so Stone Walls and the Watchtower shut lanes completely. Verdant has no answer to walls except attacking them. Breach is the default preset (D-001), so the tuning targeted breach. If Unblocked Lane is to stay a real alternative, Verdant needs an answer to walls, such as Siege on one card.
